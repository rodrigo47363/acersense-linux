🔋 Acer Care Center - Battery Protection
80% Battery Health Protection
Caps charging at 80% maximum capacity to preserve lithium-ion cell chemistry and prevent premature degradation when plugged into AC power.

💡 Why use the 80% charge limit?

Gaming laptops frequently stay plugged into power during extended gaming or compiling sessions. Maintaining Li-ion cells at 100% full state of charge with elevated temperatures drastically increases cell wear. Limiting the charge to 80% doubles battery lifespan over 2-3 years.
